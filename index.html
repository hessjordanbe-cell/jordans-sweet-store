FAQ

Quick answers.

How do I place an order?

Add candy to your cart, review it, and submit the order request form. The shop will review the request and contact you to confirm the details.

Do I pay on the website?

No. This version does not collect card information. A parent/guardian can arrange the payment method after reviewing the order.

Do you deliver?

Delivery or pickup should only be offered if the shop has a safe arrangement for it. The option can be confirmed with the customer after the request.
