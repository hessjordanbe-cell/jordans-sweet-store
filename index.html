FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

How do I place an order?
Add candy to your cart, click checkout, enter your contact information, and send the order request.
Do I pay on this website?
No. This site sends an order request. A parent/guardian confirms the final price and handles payment and fulfillment.
Can I request pickup or delivery?
Yes. Choose your preferred option during checkout. Availability and details will be confirmed after the request is received.
Can I change an order?
Send your change as soon as possible. Orders are not considered final until they are confirmed.
